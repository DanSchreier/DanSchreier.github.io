Jarvis

A private voice and text assistant that one person, its developer, runs on his own Mac and Android phone. It isn't offered to the public and has no other users.

What it does

  • Answers questions and keeps notes in the developer's own Obsidian notes.
  • Reads his Gmail and writes drafts for him to review. It never sends email.
  • Reads and updates his Google Calendar and Google Tasks, and finds and reads files in his Google Drive.
  • Controls his own home devices (Nest thermostat and doorbell alerts, lights, Chromecasts).

Why it asks for Google access

Jarvis signs in to the developer's own Google account so it can do the things above when he asks: summarize today's email, draft a reply, add a calendar event, check off a task, or find a document. It only acts on his requests, only on his account.

See the privacy policy for exactly what is accessed, where it goes, and how to revoke access.
